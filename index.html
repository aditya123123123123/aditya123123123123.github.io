ABOUT

I'm an 11th grader at Trinity School in New York. I direct AI-agent-assisted research on jazz generation, and I play lead alto sax in our Jazz Ensemble and Jazz Messengers. The research is still in progress: I compare models and simpler baselines, listen to the results, and keep track of what doesn't work.

This summer I taught responsible AI with Breakthrough New York and worked on business systems during my CloudExtel internship. I'm also contributing to Audemy's accessible audio-gaming work. Outside that, I co-lead Trinity's Sol Tutors chapter and serve on the Honor Council.
